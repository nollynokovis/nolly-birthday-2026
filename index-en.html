RULES

VALORANT

・5v5 Team Tournament — Single Elimination, Best of 1

・All Ranks Welcome, Free Team Formation

・Preliminary Matchups: Random Draw

・Maps: Each team registers 2 maps to ban. After both teams' banned maps are excluded, the map will be randomly selected.

・Choice of Attacking / Defending Side: Random Draw

SF6 1v1 Tournament

・1v1 Single Elimination

・Preliminary Round to Semifinals: Best of 1 (1 game)

・3rd Place Match & Final: Best of 3 (3 games)

・Preliminary Matchups: Random Draw

・Characters cannot be changed from the Preliminary Round to the Semifinals

・3rd Place Match & Final: the losing player may change characters

SF6 3v3 Team Tournament

・3v3 Single Elimination, Best of 1

・Health and SA Gauge Carry Over

・Teams: Participants will be divided into groups based on Rank, then randomly assigned to teams.

・Before each round, each team may decide the order of their Vanguard / Middle / Anchor players.

・Characters cannot be changed during the tournament
