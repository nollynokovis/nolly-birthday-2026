RULES

VALORANT

【Event Overview】

Event: Nolly CUP VALORANT 5v5 Team Tournament

Game: VALORANT

Dates: November 7 (Sat) – November 8 (Sun), 2026

Venue: Nolly's channel

Organizer: Nolly


【Format】

・5v5 Single Elimination, Best of 1

・Preliminary matchups will be decided by random draw

・The losing team is eliminated, and the winning team advances to the next round

・Preliminaries through Quarterfinals: If regulation ends in a 12-12 tie, the match goes to overtime, played as sudden death (same as standard matches)

・Semifinals and Final: If regulation ends in a 12-12 tie, the match goes to overtime under the Deuce rule (win by 2). The first team to lead by 2 rounds wins. Teams alternate attack and defense every overtime round. Ties are not allowed

・Each team has 2 timeouts

・Preliminaries through Quarterfinals will be played simultaneously, and the organizer will randomly select one match to stream. The Semifinals and Final will be streamed in full


【Teams】

・All ranks welcome, free team formation. Each team has 5 main players and may register up to 1 substitute

・Substitutes may only play when a main player is unable to compete due to unforeseen circumstances; tactical substitutions are not allowed

・Each participant may join only one team

・In principle, roster changes are not allowed after registration closes. In special circumstances, please contact Nolly in advance


【Map Selection】

・Each team registers 2 fixed ban maps, which apply to all of its matches in this tournament

・For each match, both teams' ban maps are excluded and 1 map is randomly selected from the remaining pool

・Ban maps can be changed until November 1, 23:59

・Once the map is decided, one randomly chosen team gets to pick its side


※After registering, you will be added to the tournament Discord channel, where the schedule and all announcements will be posted.

※On match day, the VALORANT lobby code will be posted on Discord. Please keep an eye on notifications.


【Prohibited Conduct】

・Verbal abuse, malicious attacks, or disrupting matches

・Cheating or playing on someone else's behalf

・Exploiting bugs or glitches to gain an unfair advantage

・Ghosting

・Intentional disconnection, passive play, or deliberate stalling

・Any other behavior the organizer deems to seriously compromise fair play

・All participants must also follow the basic rules of Nolly's channel and Discord community during the tournament


【Late Arrival / Disconnection】

・Each team must gather and be ready before the scheduled match time

・A team that cannot field 5 players within 10 minutes of the scheduled time will forfeit

・In the event of a disconnection or major technical issue, notify Nolly immediately. The match may be paused to wait for reconnection, depending on the situation

・If the match cannot resume within a reasonable time, the organizer will make a ruling based on the situation


※A Discord voice channel will be provided for each team. Team voice chat may be used freely, but talking with opponents through the in-game mic is prohibited (using the purple in-game mic is not allowed).

※Any situation not covered by these rules will be decided by the organizer based on fairness and tournament progress.

※The organizer reserves the right to adjust the schedule and rules according to actual circumstances. Any major changes will be announced before the tournament.

※Violations may result in a warning, a match loss, or disqualification, depending on severity.

RULES

STREET FIGHTER 6

【Event Overview】

Event: Nolly CUP STREET FIGHTER 6 Individual Tournament & 3v3 Team Tournament

Game: STREET FIGHTER 6

Dates: November 14 (Sat) – November 15 (Sun), 2026

Venue: Nolly's channel

Organizer: Nolly


【Individual Tournament】

・1v1 Single Elimination

・Preliminary Round to Semifinals: Best of 1 (1 game)

・3rd Place Match & Final: Best of 3 (3 games)

・From the Preliminary Round to the Semifinals, players must use the character registered at sign-up

・3rd Place Match & Final: the losing player may change characters

・Preliminary matchups will be decided by random draw


【3v3 Team Tournament】

・3 players per team. Health and SA Gauge carry over. Teams compete in Single Elimination, Best of 1 (1 game)

・The winner stays on to face the next opponent, and the loser leaves. The first team to defeat all three opposing players wins

・Participants will be divided into groups based on Rank, then randomly assigned to teams. Free team formation is not available

・Players must use the character registered at sign-up throughout the tournament

・Before each round, each team may decide the order of their Vanguard / Middle / Anchor players

・The order is revealed only after both teams have submitted theirs

・If the number of participants is not a multiple of 3, additional recruitment or substitutes will be arranged first. If a full team still cannot be formed, the organizer will make other arrangements


※After registering, you will be added to the tournament Discord channel, where the schedule and all announcements will be posted.

※On match day, the room password will be posted on Discord. Please keep an eye on notifications.


【Prohibited Conduct】

・Verbal abuse, malicious attacks, or disrupting matches

・Cheating or playing on someone else's behalf

・Exploiting bugs or glitches to gain an unfair advantage

・Intentional disconnection, passive play, or deliberate stalling

・Any other behavior the organizer deems to seriously compromise fair play

・All participants must also follow the basic rules of Nolly's channel and Discord community during the tournament


※Violations may result in a warning, a match loss, or disqualification, depending on severity.


【Rules】

・All matches will take place on stream

・Each participant must use the character registered at sign-up throughout the tournament (except that in the Individual Tournament's 3rd Place Match & Final, the losing player may change characters)

・Both Classic and Modern controls are allowed

・If a disconnection occurs for the first time, the match will be restarted

・If the same participant disconnects a second time in the same match, the opponent wins

・Please be ready for your match in advance as instructed by the organizer. If you cannot make it on time, let us know as early as possible

・Participants who do not respond after being called multiple times will forfeit


※Any situation not covered by these rules will be decided by the organizer based on fairness and tournament progress.

※The organizer reserves the right to adjust the schedule and rules according to actual circumstances. Any major changes will be announced before the tournament.

RULES

STREAMING & RECORDING

【Overview】

・Participants are welcome to stream or record their own point of view during Nolly CUP

・No notice or application to the organizer is required; whether to stream is up to each participant

・If you stream, please follow the rules below


【Stream Title & Description】

・Please include the tag #Nolly盃 or related keywords in your stream title

・Please state in the stream description or pinned comment that the stream is part of this event, and include the link to the official stream

・A sample description is shown below. The format is not mandatory and may be adjusted freely

This stream shows the personal perspective of a participant in "Nolly CUP 2026".

▶Organizer: Nolly https://www.youtube.com/@nollynokovis

▶Official stream: (The link will be announced before the event)

▶Event website: https://nolly-birthday-2026.pages.dev/


【Ghosting】

・Watching an opponent's stream, or obtaining an opponent's real-time information by any other means, is prohibited during the tournament

・Violators will be disqualified immediately


【Stream Delay】

・Setting a stream delay is not required

・Streaming without a delay carries a risk of being ghosted; each participant should weigh this risk and decide for themselves


【VALORANT Team Voice】

・Team voice chat may be used freely

・Talking with opponents through the in-game mic is prohibited (using the purple in-game mic is not allowed)


【Music, Images & Other Materials】

・Use of music, images and other materials is not restricted by the organizer, but participants must make sure their use complies with copyright law and the streaming platform's rules


【Stream Prohibitions】

・Do not impersonate the official stream or promote your stream in a way that may lead viewers to mistake it for the official stream

・Do not rebroadcast the official stream directly

・Respect other participants; malicious attacks, discrimination and other inappropriate remarks are prohibited


【Official Logo】

・The organizer provides the official Nolly CUP logo in two versions (with shadow / without shadow); you may freely choose either

・Logo download: https://xgf.nu/eUzo

・The logo may be used for stream thumbnails and on stream screens; use is optional

・You may freely adjust its size and position, but distorting or altering the original design is prohibited

・Do not use the logo for any purpose unrelated to this event, or in a way that makes viewers mistake your stream for the official broadcast


※If you have any questions, please contact the organizer at any time.
